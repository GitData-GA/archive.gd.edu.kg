General

A preprint is a scientific manuscript that authors post on an open platform (usually before or in parallel with the journal's peer review process). GitData Archive is an open access online archive and service for preprints.

The purpose of GitData Archive is to increase the openness and accessibility of scientific results, increase collaboration among researchers, document the origins of ideas, and inform ongoing and planned research through more timely reporting of completed studies.

By posting preprints on GitData Archive, authors have the opportunity to instantly familiarize the scientific community with the results of their research and receive feedback from colleagues before publication in the journal.

Content on GitData Archive is moderated, but not reviewed. You can search through uploaded preprints or view the latest uploaded preprints in your area of interest.

Indexing, Database Connections, and Standard

GitData Archive is indexed and connected to multiple databases to enhance discoverability, accessibility, and long-term availability of preprints:

  • OpenDOAR and ROAR: The entire repository is registered with OpenDOAR and ROAR, ensuring compliance with international repository standards and enhancing global visibility.
  • OpenAIRE: Each preprint is registered with OpenAIRE, increasing discoverability and integration with European and worldwide research infrastructures.
  • Zenodo: Every preprint is backed up on Zenodo, providing an alternative DOI tracking system and ensuring availability even if either GitData Archive or Zenodo experiences downtime.

GitData Archive is committed to widely recognized academic and publishing standards that promote openness, transparency, and accessibility:

  • Open Access: All preprints are freely accessible without embargo or subscription, aligning with global open science practices and ensuring barrier-free access to knowledge.
  • Creative Commons: Preprints are distributed under various Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licenses, allowing broad reuse with proper attribution.
